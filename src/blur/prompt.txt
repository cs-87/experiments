In `~/Documents/experiments/src/blur`, I have a POC for my watermarking algorithm.

My intention is to embed 32 bits using as few frames as possible. Right now, I am embedding a single bit per frame (actually, the same bit across `n` frames, where `n` is my temporal redundancy).

The basic idea is to select a patch (or patches) from the left half for bit 1 and run it through a high-pass filter, vice versa for bit 0 where i secelt right patch. 

The frequencies to filter are determined by the `RADIUS` parameter in `blur.py`.

Basically, I take the DCT of the patch and remove all `(x, y)` components from the DCT matrix for which:

`sqrt(x² + y²) > RADIUS`

To increase robustness, I currently use temporal redundancy, which basically means storing the same bit across `n` frames.

Detection currently uses the high-frequency energy of the patch in the left half (`one_energy`) and the high-frequency energy of the patch in the right half (`zero_energy`) for each frame.

I collect and sum the one/zero energies across all frames belonging to a particular bit position, for both the original and watermarked videos.
I then subtract the watermarked energy from the original energy to get `one_loss` and `zero_loss`. 
For that bit position, if `one_loss > zero_loss`, I determine the bit to be 1 
(although I am actually using a ratio in the current implementation).
